## Accepted intent

A signed-in user holds an analytical conversation about TheLook data:
- The AI assesses relevance and intent on every turn.
- The backend enforces every data rule: refine or analyse the stored complete result, clarify, propose a dataset or scope switch and wait for confirmation, or explain.
- All numbers are generated by the backend.

TASK-003 builds the whole workflow with fake data and AI adapters, so it needs no cloud credentials. TASK-004 adds BigQuery and Claude Sonnet 5.5.
